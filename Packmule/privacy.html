Packmule · Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Packmule is a file transfer app for iPhone by Redfern's Outpost. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no telemetry. Nothing is collected, sold or shared.

What Packmule connects to

Only the servers you add or pick, and only when you use them.

  • SMB, FTP and SFTP servers: your files move directly between your iPhone and the server. Packmule makes no other network connections.
  • Jellyfin: your Jellyfin user name and password go only to the Jellyfin server you added, to sign in and to browse, stream and download your libraries.
  • Nearby: Bonjour discovery listens for SMB, FTP and SFTP servers and other Packmules advertising on your own local network.
  • Hosting: when you start hosting, the built in FTP server serves your Packmule folder and linked folders to devices on your local network or VPN until you stop it. Hosting never runs over cellular. It is off unless you turn it on, and it can require a user name and password that you choose.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Server addresses, settings and transfer history are stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone.
  • Server passwords and the hosting password are stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • The SSH host keys of SFTP servers you connect to are remembered, so a changed server can be noticed.
  • Downloads land in Packmule's folder, which you can see in the Files app.

Photos

If you open Photos in Packmule, it asks for access to your photo library so you can browse it and copy photos and videos to a server or to the Files app. Packmule only reads the library. It never edits or deletes anything in it, and nothing is uploaded unless you send it.

Face ID

The optional app lock and the saved password reveal use Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode through iOS. Packmule never sees your face data or passcode.

Children

Packmule does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
